# Your blog — a simple guide

Welcome! This is everything you need to run your website. You never have to touch any
code. Everything happens on one screen: your **dashboard**.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin** (for example, `pradeepsingh.pages.dev/admin`).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You'll land on your dashboard, which shows how many posts you have, any comments
   waiting for you, and unread messages.

You can bookmark the `/admin` page so it's always one click away.

## Writing a post

1. Click **✎ Write a new post** (top of the dashboard) or go to **Posts → New post**.
2. Type a **title**.
3. Write your article in the big editor. The toolbar at the top gives you:
   - **B** bold, **I** italic
   - **H2 / H3** for section headings
   - a quote, bullet lists, numbered lists, and a divider line
   - **🔗** to add a link
   - **🖼 Image** to add a picture from your computer
   - **▶ YouTube** to drop in a video
4. **Adding a picture:** click **🖼 Image**, choose a photo. After it appears, the four
   arrow buttons on the right of the toolbar let you place it **left, centre, right, or
   full width**. Pictures are automatically shrunk so your pages stay fast.
5. Pick a **category** (or type a new one), and add a few **tags** separated by commas
   (like `running, books`). These help readers find related writing.
6. When you're ready:
   - **Publish** — makes it live on the site.
   - **Save draft** — keeps it private so you can finish later.

You can come back and **Edit** any post anytime, and **Unpublish** or **Delete** it.

## Comments

When someone comments, it does **not** appear on the site until you say so. This keeps
out spam.

1. Go to **Comments**. New ones are under **Awaiting review**.
2. For each one you can:
   - **Approve** — it appears publicly.
   - **Mark spam** or **Delete** — it never appears.
   - **Reply** — write a response. Your reply is posted as **you** (the author) and the
     whole conversation goes live at once.

The **Comments** menu item shows a number when something is waiting for you.

## Pages (About, Contact, and more)

**Pages** are the fixed pages in your menu, like *About*.

- Go to **Pages** to edit them, using the same editor as posts.
- Tick **Show in the site menu** to put a page in the top navigation.
- Your **Contact** page has a form; anything readers send lands in **Messages**.

## Messages and subscribers

- **Messages** — notes people send through the Contact form. Reply from your own email
  by clicking their address.
- **Subscribers** — people who signed up to hear about new posts. You're just
  collecting their emails for now; you can send a newsletter later.

## Settings

Under **Settings** you can change your site's **title**, **tagline**, your **name**
(used on replies), and the site **description**.

---

That's it. Write, publish, and reply — the rest takes care of itself. If something ever
looks broken, it's a job for whoever set this up, not for you.
