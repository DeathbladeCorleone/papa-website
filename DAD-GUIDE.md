# Your blog — a simple guide

Welcome! This is everything you need to run your website. You never have to touch any
code. Everything happens in your **dashboard**.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin** (for example, `pradeepsingh.pages.dev/admin`).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You'll land on the **Overview**: the essay you were last working on, anything waiting
   for you (new comments, messages), and a few numbers.

Bookmark the `/admin` page so it's always one click away. The dark menu on the left takes
you everywhere else.

## Writing an essay

Click **Write an essay** (top left). You get a quiet page that looks just like your
website.

1. Type the **title** where it says *Title*, and if you like a one-line **introduction**
   under it.
2. Click below and start writing. **You don't need to save** — a draft saves itself a
   moment after you stop typing. The top of the screen says *Saved just now*.
3. The **toolbar** gives you headings, **bold**, *italic*, underline, highlight, links,
   lists and quotes. Select some words and a small black bar also appears right above
   them with the same buttons (and left / centre / right alignment).
4. **Quick insert:** on an empty line, type **/** (or click the **+** on the left) and
   choose: Picture, Gallery, Quote, Note box, List, YouTube video, Table or Divider line.

### Pictures

- Click **Picture** in the toolbar (or type **/picture**). Choose a photo from your
  computer, or one you used before. You can also simply **drag a photo** from your
  computer onto the page, or paste one.
- Click on a picture (or point at it) and a black bar appears on it:
  - the four layout buttons put it **left** or **right** with text around it,
    **centred**, or **wide**;
  - **S / M / L** change the size;
  - **Description** — a few words about the photo, for blind readers and Google;
  - **Replace** swaps in a different photo;
  - **Remove** takes it out of the essay. (The photo itself stays in **Photos**.)
- Type a **caption** in the line under the picture.
- **Gallery** puts several photos in a neat grid; you can add, reorder or remove photos
  in it the same way.

Big photos are shrunk automatically, so your pages stay fast.

### Essay settings (the panel on the right)

Click the **panel button** (top right) to show or hide it. Here you set the **category**,
**tags**, the **cover picture** (shown at the top of the essay, on the home page and when
shared on WhatsApp), whether it's the **big essay on the home page**, and what Google shows.

### Publishing

Click **Publish…**. Choose **Right now**, or **Later** and pick a date and time — the
essay will appear on its own at that moment. A small checklist reminds you if the title,
cover picture or category is missing.

Once an essay is published, changes are **not** shown to readers until you click
**Update**. To take it down, use **Take off the website** in the panel — it goes back to
being a draft.

### Earlier versions

Changed your mind? In the panel, open **Earlier versions**, pick one, and click
**Restore this version**. Your current text is kept too, so you can always go back.

If your computer or internet ever stops mid-sentence, open the essay again — the editor
will offer to **bring back** anything that didn't get saved.

## Comments

When someone comments, it does **not** appear on the site until you say so.

1. Go to **Comments**. New ones are under **Waiting for you**.
2. For each one: **Approve**, **Reply** (your reply is posted as you, and approving
   happens at the same time), **Spam**, or **Delete**.

The menu shows a number when something is waiting.

## Pages (About, Contact, and more)

**Pages** are the fixed pages, like *About*. They use the same editor as essays. The
first lines of your About page appear in "About me" on the home page.

## Your website

- **Home page** — choose which essay is the big one at the top (or let it always be the
  newest), switch sections on or off, put them in a different order, and rename their
  headings. In the **About me** row, **Add photo** puts your portrait beside it (until you
  do, it shows your initials).
- **Menu & topics** — put your pages in the order you want in the top menu, and rename,
  merge or delete categories and tags.
- **Photos** — every picture you've uploaded. Click one to add a description, see which
  essays use it, copy its link, or delete it. *Not used anywhere* shows photos you can
  safely clear out.
- **Settings** — the website's name and tagline, your name and photo, and the short
  description Google uses.

## Messages and subscribers

- **Messages** — notes sent through the Contact page. Click **Reply by email**.
- **Subscribers** — people who left their email. Nothing is sent to them yet;
  **Download list** when you're ready for a newsletter.

---

That's it. Write, publish, and reply — the rest takes care of itself. If something ever
looks broken, it's a job for whoever set this up, not for you.
